Double Barcode Extraction Summary

5′ UMI

Raw UMI Count: The total number of unique molecular identifiers (UMIs) detected before any clustering or error correction.
Total UMI Clusters: The number of distinct UMI groups after clustering similar UMIs to account for sequencing and PCR errors.
Chao1 Estimate: A statistical estimate of the true number of unique UMIs, accounting for undetected or rare UMIs.
Updated Chao1 Estimate: A refined Chao1 estimate that takes into account that we used paired-end reads, where there is a significant chance of reading the same UMI twice.
Shannon's Entropy: A single number quantitative measure of UMI diversity, reflecting both the number and distribution of UMIs.
